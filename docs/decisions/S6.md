## Question

§2 calls an asset "any other file in the store", and §3.3 says that `.vmd/` and dotfiles are not records, so the two word their
treatment differently. The read commands (#84, its first open question) leave them out of `ls` and `grep`, and `cat` reads them.
Are `.vmd/` and dotfiles assets?

## Options

- **(a) They are ignored, neither records nor assets.** Chosen, as recommended. `ls` and `grep` leave them out; `cat` still reads
  them when named, so configurations can be inspected.
- **(b) They are assets**, as §2's "any other file in the store" reads.

## Decision

(a), as part of card S's option (a), accepting every recommendation.

## Why

The card's reason: `cat` still reads them when named, so a configuration can be inspected. Card S as a whole keeps the output
"cheap in tokens".

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
