## Question

Item 1 of card H2, from #52 Q2. §4.1 restricts the data model "as I-JSON restricts it", and RFC 7493, section 2.1, forbids
noncharacters as well as surrogates, but the table and Appendix D name only unpaired surrogates. Is a noncharacter in a string,
such as U+FFFF in `{"s": "￿"}`, valid?

## Options

- **(a) Valid, and §4.1 notes the departure from I-JSON.** Chosen, as recommended.
- **(b) An error, with a new code**, the other reading #52 Q2 names.

## Decision

(a), with card H2's option (a), accepting every recommendation. Noncharacters are valid, and §4.1 records the departure from
I-JSON (RFC 7493, section 2.1), citing Unicode Corrigendum #9.

## Why

The reason in #52 Q2: noncharacters are Unicode scalar values, and Unicode Corrigendum #9 allows them in interchange (the PR's
author noted that they had not re-checked the corrigendum; the draft cites it). Card H2's reason for accepting every
recommendation: each keeps vmd strict and its output readable back, and none contradicts an earlier decision. Why (b) lost: Not
recorded.
