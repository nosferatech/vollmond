## Question

The validator does not know that `x-vmd-list` evaluates the items, so `unevaluatedItems: false` beside it would fail valid data.
What does vmd do with such a schema?

## Options

- **(a) `schema-invalid`.** Chosen, as recommended. §9.3 says so.
- **(b) Compile the per-key schemas into the schema the validator sees.** Brings back the compilation that the design's native
  evaluation of keyed lists avoids ([i2-validate-1](i2-validate-1.md)).

## Decision

(a).

## Why

The card's reason: "Simple, and catches the trap at load." (b) brings back the compilation the design avoids.
