## Question

A gap that [U12](U12.md) opens (§20's question 10). Under U12, readers refuse the records of a collection whose schema cannot be
used. Three operations do not say what they do then:

- `rename` and `mv` (§13.4) already refuse with `unreadable-records` when records with structural errors exist, because
  "references in those records cannot be found". The same holds here: typed `x-vmd-ref` strings cannot be found without the
  schema.
- A write into such a record (§9.4).
- The index (§14.2) lists only structurally broken records without nodes.

## Options

- **(a) Refuse, like unreadable records.** Chosen, as recommended. `rename` and `mv` refuse with `unreadable-records`; a write
  into such a record is refused with the schema's code; §14.2 lists such records without nodes.
- **(b) Allow, with a warning.** `rename` proceeds and warns that typed references in that collection were not updated; writes go
  through unvalidated.

## Decision

(a): `rename` and `mv` refuse with `unreadable-records` while a collection's schema cannot be used, a write into one of its
records is refused with the schema's code, and the index lists such records without nodes.

**Agent's reading, confirmed by the owner on [W3](W3.md) (item 4).** The card does not say where the write is refused. Draft v0.12 refuses it in every
mode of §9.4, by whichever part validates the write, the writing client's library included, since the write could not be
validated in any of them.

## Why

The card's reason: "It is §13.4's own reason, applied consistently; (b) would leave stale references silently."
