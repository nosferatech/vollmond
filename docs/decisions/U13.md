## Question

§9.1 says a collection's schemas are in `.vmd/schema/`. vampiredb's `docs/` is read with `--config examples/vampiredb/config.yaml`
([K3](K3.md), [O1](O1.md)) and has no `.vmd/`, so under §9.1 it could have no schemas without modifying vampiredb. Where are a
configuration's schemas?

## Options

- **(a) In `schema/` beside the configuration file.** Chosen, as recommended. The same as today for a store's own configuration
  (`.vmd/schema/`), and for the suite's `config` input; `examples/vampiredb/schema/` for the trial. §9.1 and §12.1 say so.
- **(b) Always the store's `.vmd/schema/`.** vampiredb's trial cannot have schemas without changing vampiredb.
- **(c) A `schemas` member names the directory.** More flexible, one more configuration member.

## Decision

(a).

## Why

The card's reason: "No change for normal stores, and it lets the I2.7 trial leave vampiredb unmodified." (b) gives a store read
with `--config` no schemas.

Why (c) lost: Not recorded.
