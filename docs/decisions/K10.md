## Question

Question 10 of the phase I1 design (#59), item 10 of card K. §12.2 asks `ls` to say how many records it could not read, which
needs a parse of every record; parsing all of vampiredb took about 1.5 s warm, as the design's review measured. How does `ls`
count them?

## Options

- **(a) Count over the whole listing.**
- **(b) Count only the records on the printed page, and say so.** Chosen for I1, as recommended.
- **(c) Count from the index, from task I2.5.** Chosen for once the index exists, as recommended.

## Decision

(b) in I1 and (c) once the index exists, through card K's option (b), which accepted every recommendation but question 3's: `ls`
counts unreadable records only on the page it prints, and says so; the index of task I2.5 gives the full count. It is an
implementation choice that the design records.

## Why

The design's reason: (b) in I1, so `ls` stays fast; (a) needs a parse of every record, about 1.5 s for all of vampiredb.

The design's section 5 (#62, `369b05a`); the CLI foundation (#74, `9d9c7bb`).
