## Question

Item 11 of card I1, from #57's open questions: what are the default `targets` of an `x-vmd-ref` that omits them?

## Options

- **(a) Any node in the store.** Chosen, as recommended.

Other options: Not recorded.

## Decision

Card I1's option (a), accepting every recommendation: an `x-vmd-ref` without `targets` allows any node in the store (§9.3).

## Why

The card's reason: it "is what a missing constraint means in JSON Schema".

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.

## Applied in

Draft v0.6 (#58, `c3879b7`), §9.3. [J6](J6.md) widened it in Draft v0.7 to any target, assets and directories included.
