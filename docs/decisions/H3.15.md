## Question

§5.3 defined `$body` as "the text after the heading (and the data block)", and a root without a title heading has no heading.
What is its `$body`? The Markdown fixtures (#54, question 15) asked with front matter, an intro paragraph, then `## Notes`.
Seven included cases already rested on one reading.

## Options

- **(a) The text from after the front matter, or the start of the file, up to the first heading.** Chosen, as recommended, and
  §5.3 says so.
- **(b) Nothing,** so such a root has no `$body`.

## Decision

(a), as card H3's option (a), accepting every recommendation. The root's `$body` without a title heading is the text after the
front matter, or from the start of the file, up to the first heading.

## Why

The PR's reason: without (a), the intro of every record without a title would be lost, which is what (b) does. The card's note
for all sixteen items: each recommendation keeps vmd strict and its output readable back, and none contradicts an earlier
decision.
