## Question

Item 9 of card H2, from #52 Q10's second point. A plain key such as `yes: 1` is a plain scalar that YAML 1.1 readers misread, but
it is unclear which node the warning's `at` names. Where does the warning for an ambiguous key go?

## Options

- **(a) On the mapping that holds the key.** Chosen, as recommended.

Other options: Not recorded.

## Decision

(a), with card H2's option (a), accepting every recommendation. A key's ambiguity warning goes on its mapping (§4.4, Appendix D).

## Why

The card's reason: a key has no path of its own. Card H2's reason for accepting every recommendation: each keeps vmd strict and
its output readable back, and none contradicts an earlier decision.
