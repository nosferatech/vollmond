## Question

Item 10 of card I1, from #57's open questions: a `%YAML` directive in front matter cannot be followed by its `---` without closing
the front matter, so §4.4's "in front matter" case cannot arise as written. What does a lone directive there report?

## Options

- **(a) A `syntax-error`, since `%` cannot start a YAML value, and §4.4 drops its front-matter case.** Chosen, as recommended.
- **(b) `yaml-version-unsupported`.** Named by #57's question as the other possible code.

## Decision

Card I1's option (a), accepting every recommendation: a `%YAML` line in front matter is a `syntax-error`, and §4.4 drops its
front-matter case (§4.4, Appendix D).

## Why

The card's reason: the line cannot be a directive, because the `---` it needs closes the front matter, and `%` cannot start a
YAML value.

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.

## Applied in

Draft v0.6 (#58, `c3879b7`), §4.4 and Appendix D.
