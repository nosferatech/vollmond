## Question

Applying [H2.7](H2.7.md), the first version of Draft v0.5 claimed that decimal had no external standard and gave it vmd's own
lexical space, `-?(0|[1-9][0-9]*)(\.[0-9]+)?`, which accepts `1.50` and `-0` but not `.5` or `+1`. The review of #57 found it
narrower than H2.7 intended. The session asked: "Decimal has no format of its own in the standards §4.3 cites. Your H2.7 answer
was to accept the full form a standard allows. Which forms should a decimal value accept?"

## Options

- **(a) XML Schema's decimal.** Chosen, as recommended. Accept what XML Schema 1.1 allows: `.5`, `+1`, `1.`, `01` and `1.50`.
  The writer still emits the canonical form (no `+`, no leading zeros, no trailing dot). Consistent with H2.7.
- **(b) Only the canonical form.** `-?(0|[1-9][0-9]*)(\.[0-9]+)?` as in the PR: rejects `.5`, `+1`, `1.` and `01`. Narrower than
  Draft v0.4 was.

## Decision

(a). Decimal accepts XML Schema 1.1 Part 2's `decimal` (section 3.3.3.1, regular expression
`(\+|-)?([0-9]+(\.[0-9]*)?|\.[0-9]+)`), so `.5`, `+1`, `1.`, `01` and `1.50` are accepted. vmd's
`-?(0|[1-9][0-9]*)(\.[0-9]+)?` stays as the canonical form the writer emits. Two points are the agent's reading: that vmd applies
no whitespace collapsing before the lexical space, as XML does; and the mapping §4.3 gives from an accepted form to the written
one (drop `+`, drop leading zeros, add `0` before a leading point, drop a bare trailing point, keep trailing zeros since they can
carry precision, write `-0` as `0`).

## Why

The session's reasons, from the question's options: (a) is consistent with H2.7's full lexical space, and the writer keeps the
canonical form; (b) is narrower than Draft v0.4 was.
