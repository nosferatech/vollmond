## Question

Is `notes.MD` a record? The value model (#66) raised the letter case of record extensions among its readings for the project
owner, noted in #10, and took `a.MD` as not a record meanwhile.

## Options

- **(a) No: extensions are compared exactly**, `.md`, `.json`, `.yaml` and `.yml`, like the rest of a path. Chosen, as
  recommended.

Other options: Not recorded.

## Decision

(a), with card L's option (a), accepting every recommendation. Record extensions are compared exactly (`.md`, `.json`, `.yaml`,
`.yml`), like the rest of a path, so `a.MD` is an asset, not a record (§3.1), with a case in a new suite topic, `paths`.

## Why

The card's reason: extensions are compared like the rest of a path, which is exact. For the card as a whole: each default is the
safe one, and the reviewers checked them.

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.

## Applied in

Draft v0.8 (#67, `26a1a55`), §3.1, with the case `paths/extensions`: `notes.MD` and `data.Json` are assets, so `check` reports
nothing.
