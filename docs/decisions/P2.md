## Question

CommonMark lets an unclosed fence run to the end of the file, and YAML then reads every following `## heading` as a comment, so
whole sections vanish without an issue. What is an unclosed `yaml data` fence? The question came from the independent review of
the Markdown parser (#79).

## Options

- **(a) A `syntax-error`**, as unclosed front matter already is ([H3.4](H3.4.md)). Chosen, as recommended.
- **(b) Keep the draft as it is.** The fence runs to the end of the file, and the sections after it vanish without an issue.

## Decision

(a), as part of card P's option (a), accepting every recommendation: an unclosed data fence is a `syntax-error`, as unclosed front
matter is.

**Agent's reading, for the owner to confirm.** A misplaced data block, or a second one after a section's data block, whose fence
is not closed gets the `syntax-error` too, at the root or at the section, beside its `data-block-misplaced`. Appendix D's
`syntax-error` row, "the record, or the data block's section", supports it, and #94 implements it; Draft v0.12 aligns §5.3 with
it.

## Why

The card's reason: the rest of card P "picks the reading that loses no data silently". (b) makes whole sections vanish without an
issue.
