## Question

Question 8 of the phase I1 design (#59), item 8 of card K. §6.3 starts from a heading's visible text, but a data title is a plain
string. How is a JSON or YAML section's `$title` read for its slug?

## Options

- **(a) As the content of an ATX heading, `## ` and the title.** Chosen, as recommended. A section keeps its key whether it is
  written in Markdown or YAML.
- **(b) As a paragraph's inline content.**

## Decision

(a), through card K's option (b), which accepted every recommendation but question 3's. The slug of a JSON or YAML section's
`$title` is computed as if the title were the content of an ATX heading, `## ` followed by the title, so a section keeps its key
across formats (§5.5). A title that would not read back that way, ending in a closing sequence such as ` #`, made only of `#`
characters (which the review of the round added, since `## #` reads as an empty heading), with white space at either end, or
holding an anchor element, is not representable as Markdown (§5.8).

## Why

The design's reason: so that a section keeps its key across formats. It showed a gap in §5.8, closed with the decision: a title
that does not read back from `## ` and itself should be not representable.

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
