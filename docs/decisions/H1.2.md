## Question

Item 2 of card H1, from #53 Q1. Appendix D gives no node (`at` null) for `address-malformed`, `address-not-singular`,
`address-not-found` and `address-ambiguous`, and the README says `path` is the record the issue is about, and null for an issue
that belongs to no record. For `notes/design.md#nothing` resolved as singular, the issue's `path` could be `notes/design.md` or
null. And no code was named for an address whose record does not exist (`missing.md#x`). The answer blocked every failing
`resolve` case.

## Options

- **(a) `path` is the record the address names, `at` is null, and a missing record is `address-not-found`.** Chosen, as
  recommended.
- **(b) `path` is null**, the other reading #53 Q1 names.

## Decision

(a), with card H1's option (a), accepting every recommendation. For `address-malformed`, `address-not-singular`,
`address-not-found` and `address-ambiguous`, `path` is the record the address names, and `at` is null. An address whose record
does not exist is `address-not-found`, as a singular address and as a selector (§7.4, Appendix D).

## Why

Card H1's reason for accepting every recommendation: each keeps vmd strict and its output readable back, and none contradicts an
earlier decision. Why (b) lost: Not recorded.

## Applied in

Draft v0.5 (#57, `d0d1424`), §7.4 and Appendix D, with the failing `resolve` cases that had waited on it.
