## Question

In `## *A <a id="d"></a>*`, removing the anchor element breaks the markup and leaves the `$title` `*A *`. Where is an anchor
element recognized in a heading? The question came from the independent review of the Markdown parser (#79).

## Options

- **(a) Only among the heading's direct children.** Chosen, as recommended. Elsewhere, inside emphasis or a link, it is
  `anchor-element-invalid`.
- **(b) Keep the draft as it is.** The element is removed wherever it stands, and the title can come out broken, as `*A *`.

## Decision

(a), as part of card P's option (a), accepting every recommendation: the anchor element is recognized only among the heading's
direct children, and one elsewhere in the heading is `anchor-element-invalid`.

## Why

The card's reason for card P: it "picks the reading that loses no data silently". (b) leaves a broken `$title`.
