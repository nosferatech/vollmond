## Question

Question 11 of the phase I1 design (#59), item 11 of card K: where does the TypeScript conformance runner live?

## Options

- **(a) A private `js/packages/conformance`.** Chosen, as recommended.
- **(b) A hidden `vmd` subcommand.**
- **(c) A Vitest suite in `cli`.**

## Decision

(a), through card K's option (b), which accepted every recommendation but question 3's: the runner is the private package
`js/packages/conformance`. It is an implementation choice that the design records, and the plan's §1.1 follows it.

## Why

The design's reason: (a) keeps it out of the published command and lets it import `core` alone.

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.

The design's sections 1.3 and 6 and the plan's §1.1 (#62, `369b05a`); the runner in #65 (`e67a935`).
