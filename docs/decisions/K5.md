## Question

Question 5 of the phase I1 design (#59), item 5 of card K: the specification says nothing on a record that is not valid UTF-8.
What is it?

## Options

- **(a) A `syntax-error` at `""`.** Chosen, as recommended, with a test case in each format.
- **(b) A code of its own.**

## Decision

(a), through card K's option (b), which accepted every recommendation but question 3's: invalid UTF-8 is a `syntax-error` at
`""`, in every format (§5.1), with a case in each.

## Why

The card's reason for the whole card: "The reviewer checked each recommendation and called them sound."

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.

## Applied in

Draft v0.7 (#61, `c3032a6`), §5.1, with a suite case in each format.
