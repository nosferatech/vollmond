## Question

Open point 8 of Draft v0.2: what happens to references that leave the store? Draft v0.2 had a store registry and an `unverified`
status for them.

## Options

- **(a) Draft v0.2's store registry, with an `unverified` status** for references into other stores.
- **(b) Ordinary, untracked links.** Chosen. A user who wants tracking brings every collection into one store, preferably on one
  backend.

## Decision

(b). Links that leave the store are ordinary, untracked links. Reference tracking and rewriting need every collection in one
store, preferably on one backend, since there are no transactions across backends.

## Why

The owner's reasons: how outside references are written and interpreted is the user's problem, and tracking across backends would
need transactions that do not exist. Why (a) lost beyond that: Not recorded.
