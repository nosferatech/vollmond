## Question

A `$ref` object with other members, such as `{"$ref": "x.md", "note": 1}`, is `ref-malformed` at the object. Are its other
members still checked? The value model (#66) raised it among its readings for the project owner, noted in #10, and applied the
recommended reading meanwhile.

## Options

- **(a) Its other members are still checked and their issues reported.** Chosen, as recommended. It follows C2's aim of one run
  showing everything, and the spec says so.

Other options: Not recorded.

## Decision

(a), with card L's option (a), accepting every recommendation. A `$ref` object with other members is `ref-malformed` at the
object, and its other members are still checked and their issues reported, as [C2](C2.md) asks (§8.1, Appendix D), with a case.

## Why

The card's reason: it follows C2's aim of one run showing everything to fix. For the card as a whole: each default is the safe
one, and the reviewers checked them.

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
