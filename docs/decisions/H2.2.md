## Question

Item 2 of card H2, from #52 Q3. A YAML surrogate pair written as two `\u` escapes, `s: "😀"`: YAML 1.2.2, section 5.7,
calls `\u` an "escaped 16-bit Unicode character" and does not say that two escapes combine into one character. The `yaml` package
(2.9.1) gives the single character U+1F600, while a Python reader probably gives two lone surrogates (inferred, not measured).
Is it one character or two?

## Options

- **(a) One valid character, as in JSON.** Chosen, as recommended. A Python implementation would have to combine the pair.
- **(b) Two `unpaired-surrogate` errors**, the other reading #52 Q3 names.

## Decision

(a), with card H2's option (a), accepting every recommendation. A YAML surrogate-pair escape is one character, as in JSON, so a
reader whose YAML library does not combine the two `\u` escapes must combine them itself (§4.1).

## Why

The reason in #52 Q3 and on the card: one character, "the same as JSON". Card H2's
reason for accepting every recommendation: each keeps vmd strict and its output readable back, and none contradicts an earlier
decision. Why (b) lost: Not recorded.
