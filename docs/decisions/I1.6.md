## Question

Item 6 of card I1, from #57's open questions: [H4.6](H4.6.md) makes two anchor elements in one heading a structural error, but
covers headings only. What about two `<a id>` elements at the start of one block?

## Options

- **(a) A structural error, `anchor-element-invalid`, as H4.6 decided for headings.** Chosen, as recommended.

Other options: Not recorded.

## Decision

Card I1's option (a), accepting every recommendation: two `<a id>` at the start of one block are a structural
`anchor-element-invalid`, as H4.6 is for headings (§6.2).

## Why

The card's reason: the same rule as H4.6 for headings. For the whole card: each recommendation "keeps vmd strict without rejecting
data that is plainly fine".

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.

Draft v0.6 (#58, `c3879b7`), §6.2.
