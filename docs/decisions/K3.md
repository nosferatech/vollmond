## Question

Question 3 of the phase I1 design (#59), item 3 of card K. I1 reads only `vmd` from the configuration. vampiredb has no
`.vmd/config.yaml`, and the I1.11 demonstration runs on its checkout, which is never modified. How does the demonstration get a
configuration?

## Options

- **(a) Bring `--config PATH`, planned for task I2.1, into task I1.8.** Recommended. The flag is small, and the demonstration then
  runs on the real checkout.
- **(b) Run the demonstration on a copy with a configuration added.**
- **(c) The owner's change: (a), with the demonstration store being vampiredb's `docs/` only, not the whole checkout, and
  eternal-circle brought into vmd with its own configuration and made compliant.** Chosen.

## Decision

(c), through card K's option (b), accepting every recommendation except what the note says. As the design's section 9 records it:
the demonstration store is vampiredb's `docs/`, not the whole checkout, read with `--config PATH`, which comes into task I1.8 so
that vampiredb is not modified. eternal-circle becomes a vmd store with its own `.vmd/config.yaml`, committed in that repository and
made compliant through the importer of I3.7; it gets no trial configuration in this repository. The answer is an implementation
choice that the design records, and it changed the plan's §1.4 and tasks I1.8, I1.11, I2.7 and I3.7.

## Why

The design's reason for `--config`: the flag is small, and the demonstration then runs on the real checkout. The owner's reason
for narrowing it to `docs/` is not recorded beyond the note.

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
