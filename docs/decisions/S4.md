## Question

Under `--json`, `get` returns the value, as §5.10 and §12.2 say, while the command's default form is the source, which is then not
reachable under `--json`; and `--max-chars` cuts nothing under `--json` (#84, its fifth and sixth open questions, the sixth asking
whether it should cut a string value). What does `get` give under `--json`?

## Options

- **(a) Add `--source`, to get the source text under `--json`, and keep `--max-chars` text-only.** Chosen, as recommended.
- **(b) Keep the draft as it is.** The source form cannot be reached under `--json`.
- **(c) Let `--max-chars` cut a string value under `--json`**, as #84's sixth question asked.

## Decision

(a), as part of card S's option (a), accepting every recommendation.

## Why

The card's reason: an agent asking for JSON wants all of it, so `--max-chars` stays text-only, and `--source` makes the source form
reachable under `--json`, which (b) does not. Card S as a whole keeps the output "safe for a terminal, cheap in tokens, and exact
for programs".
