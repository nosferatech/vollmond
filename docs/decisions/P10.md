## Question

A paragraph's block-anchor range is trimmed of trailing white space, but a list item's is not: the Markdown parser (#79, its
seventh open reading) ends a list item at the end of its last line, trailing spaces included. Should the two agree?

## Options

- **(a) Trim both.** Chosen, as recommended.
- **(b) Keep #79's interim reading.** A paragraph's range is trimmed and a list item's is not.

## Decision

(a), as part of card P's option (a), accepting every recommendation: both kinds of block anchor have their ranges trimmed of
trailing white space.

## Why

Not recorded. The card gives no reason for this item beyond the one for card P as a whole, that it "picks the reading that loses no
data silently".
