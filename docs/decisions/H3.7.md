## Question

§5.3 said any top-level key beginning with `$` in a data block is `dollar-member`, while Appendix D gave `dollar-member` only for
`$key` and for section members out of their place, and `feature-unsupported` for another `$` member in a data block; two fixture
authors disagreed too (#54, question 6). The address fixtures asked what `$anchor` and `$tags` at the top of front matter are
(#53, question 11): the root's anchor, a `dollar-member` error as in a data block, or `feature-unsupported`. What is a `$` key
in front matter and in a data block?

## Options

- **(a) Appendix D's split, in both places.** Chosen, as recommended. Section members (`$title`, `$anchor`, `$tags`, `$body`,
  `$sections`) and `$key` are `dollar-member`, since headings and `<a>` carry them; `$schema` is allowed in front matter only;
  any other `$` name is `feature-unsupported`, per the owner's versioning rule; §5.3 is fixed to match.
- **(b) `dollar-member` for every `$` member at the top of front matter** (#53, question 11's recommendation), so the title
  heading is the one place for the root's title, anchor and tags.
- **(c) `$anchor` at the top of front matter is the root's anchor,** possibly next to an `<a id>` on the title heading.

## Decision

(a), as card H3's option (a), accepting every recommendation. Section members (`$title`, `$anchor`, `$tags`, `$body`,
`$sections`) and `$key` are `dollar-member`; `$schema` is allowed in front matter only, as the root's; any other `$` name is
`feature-unsupported`. §5.3 now agrees with Appendix D. Card H3.7 merged #54 Q6 and #53 Q11; its recommendation allows `$schema`
in front matter, unlike #54 Q6's.

## Why

The card's and PR's reasons: unknown names are `feature-unsupported` so that a newer minor version can add one (§9.1,
[G3a](G3a.md)), and section members are `dollar-member` since a Markdown section's title, anchor, tags, body and children have
their own syntax. #53's recommendation (b) kept the title heading as the one place for the root's title, anchor and tags, which
(a) keeps for section members while letting `$schema` through. Why (c) lost: Not recorded. The card's note for all sixteen items:
each recommendation keeps vmd strict and its output readable back, and none contradicts an earlier decision.
