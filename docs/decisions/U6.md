## Question

The configuration's `issues` sets the severity of an issue code (§9.1, from the owner's note on [C7](C7.md)). Appendix D already
says that structural and operation codes cannot be configured and that a validation error stays an error ([G6](G6.md)). Open was
what an entry does to `duplicate-key`, `duplicate-anchor`, `path-invalid`, `path-case-conflict` and `not-representable`, which
are errors in some contexts and warnings in others.

## Options

- **(a) The entry applies to the warnings only.** Chosen, as recommended. It can silence or raise the warning occurrences; the
  error occurrences stay errors, as G6 keeps them.
- **(b) The entry sets every occurrence.** An entry could lower an error that G6 keeps as an error.

## Decision

(a): an `issues` entry for one of these codes applies to its warning occurrences only, and never touches its error occurrences.
Appendix D's "Configuring severities" states it.

## Why

The card's reason: "Keeps G6's rule that configuration never lowers an error." The card's consequence for (b), not a reason it
gives against it: an entry could lower an error that G6 keeps as an error.
