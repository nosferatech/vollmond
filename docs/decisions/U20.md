## Question

The configuration file has a 1 MiB bound (task I1.8, a deviation recorded in #17); schema files have none.

## Options

- **(a) At most 1 MiB.** Chosen, as recommended. §9.1 states both bounds.
- **(b) No bound.** As stated.

## Decision

(a): a schema file is at most 1 MiB, the bound the configuration file has, and §9.1 states both.

## Why

The card's reason: "A larger schema is a mistake or an attack on a validating service."

The bound limits what reading a schema file costs, and does not protect a validating service's time: a schema of 109 bytes makes
validating a record of 146 bytes take seconds, doubling with each level of its nesting (#111), and a crafted schema under the
bound can take minutes to load. [AE](AE.md) (Draft v0.15) says so in §9.2, and leaves the time budget to the service.

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
