## Question

What is a `yaml data` fence as the first block of a record without a title heading, after the front matter or with none? The
Markdown fixtures (#54, question 8) asked.

## Options

- **(a) Prose,** read literally, since the fence follows no heading, which gives a root `$body` that §5.8 cannot write back.
- **(b) `data-block-misplaced` at the root,** as after a title heading. Chosen, as recommended.

## Decision

(b), as card H3's option (a), accepting every recommendation. A data fence as the first block of a record without a title
heading is `data-block-misplaced` at the root.

## Why

The card's reason: front matter is the root's data block. The PR adds that the root's fields come only from the front matter and
that the author meant data; (a) gives a `$body` that §5.8 cannot write back. The card's note for all sixteen items: each
recommendation keeps vmd strict and its output readable back, and none contradicts an earlier decision.

## Applied in

Draft v0.5 (#57, `d0d1424`), §5.3 and Appendix D. P6 later made an unknown `<word> data` fence in this place
`feature-unsupported`.
