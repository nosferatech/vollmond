## Question

Item 9 of card I1, from #57's open questions: what do references into a record that cannot be read, because it has a structural
error, report when `refs` runs over the whole store?

## Options

- **(a) A warning, `ref-target-unreadable`.** Chosen, as recommended. The structural error is already reported, and the reference
  may be fine once it is fixed.
- **(b) `ref-dangling`.**

## Decision

Card I1's option (a), accepting every recommendation. A reference into a record with a structural error gives a new warning,
`ref-target-unreadable`, not `ref-dangling` (§8.5, Appendix D, suite README). Its status in `refs` results and the index, beside
`ok`, `dangling`, `ambiguous` and `aliased`, was not given and was left open to the owner (§20, open question 7); meanwhile
`check` reports it.

## Why

The card's reason for (a) over `ref-dangling`: the structural error is already reported, and the reference may be fine once it is
fixed.

## Applied in

Draft v0.6 (#58, `c3879b7`), §8.5, §20 and Appendix D. [J3](J3.md) gave `refs` the status `unreadable` in Draft v0.7, closing
the open question.
