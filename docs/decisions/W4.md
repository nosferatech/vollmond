## Question

Draft v0.13 (#102) applies the owner's answers to cards W3, Z1, Z2 and Z3. The card: "Where your W3 and Z answers left a detail
open, Draft v0.13's author decided it and labelled it in the records as "Agent's reading, for the owner to confirm". Confirming
makes them decided; any you reject becomes its own card." The card lists ten:

1. FIFOs in listings ([W3](W3.md)): `list` and `grep` leave a FIFO in the store out, with a `storage-failed` error.
2. Ignored paths (W3): a FIFO at a path the store ignores raises nothing in a listing; reading it by name still fails.
3. Sockets and devices (W3) are treated like FIFOs, inside the store and beside a `--config` file.
4. Where the body-text error sits ([Z1](Z1.md)): a JSON or YAML record whose `$body` passes a Markdown limit gets its
   `syntax-error` at the record's root, as a data `$title` does.
5. Every section's body (Z1): the limit holds in every section's `$body`, not only the root's.
6. Record alias chains stop at the first path that exists ([Z2](Z2.md)), which is what the references code does.
7. Anchor alias chains stop at the first live name in the same way (Z2). The card: "Card Z2 said chains are followed "to the end,
   the same way record aliases are"; this reads "the same way" as deciding it. A live name midway is your F8 question about
   reused names again."
8. Old paths count for current links too (Z2): an anchor alias recorded under `a.md` also applies to a link written as `b.md#old`
   once `a.md` has moved to `b.md`.
9. Reference warnings for section keys (the decision log of Draft v0.13): `ref-derived-anchor` and `ref-derived-repeat` also fire
   for a link that reaches a JSON or YAML section only by the key made from its `$title`. The card: "The I2 design (§5.6) already
   said so; no card did."
10. Record links: W3 marks [X1](X1.md)'s FIFO part as superseded, and Z1 marks [Y1](Y1.md) and [Y2](Y2.md), since each extends
    the earlier rule.

The card: "Left to the alias design (#48), and not decided here: which alias counts when several have the same old name, and an
old path that a live record has again."

## Options

- **(a) Confirm all ten.** Chosen, as recommended. The card: "The labels go in the next draft. Nothing in the code changes."
- **(b) Confirm, except what the note says.** The card: "Each exception becomes its own card."
- **(c) Discuss first.** The card: "The readings stay labelled."

## Decision

(a): all ten readings are confirmed. The records that hold them (W3, Z1 and Z2) and the decision log's note on the reference
warnings say so; the two questions the card leaves to #48 stay open there.

## Why

The card's reason: "Each follows from your answer or from the design, and 6 to 8 match what the references code already does.
Item 7 is the one to look at twice."

Why (b) and (c) lost: Not recorded.
