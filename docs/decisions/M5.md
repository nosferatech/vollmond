## Question

§12.2 asks every listing to say how many items there are in all. That is free for `list`, but `grep` would have to scan every
file. The storage contract and filesystem backend (#68) noted that its pages carried no count, and left the counts to the CLI's
pull requests. What do `list` and `grep` count?

## Options

- **(a) `list` gives an exact count; `grep` gives an estimate marked as such**, as the draft already does for query totals.
  Chosen, as recommended.
- **(b) Exact counts for both, as §12.2 asked of every listing.** `grep` would have to scan every file.

## Decision

(a), with card M's option (a), accepting every recommendation. `list` gives an exact count of what remains, and `grep` an estimate
marked as one, as query totals are (§11.2, §12.2). The card cited §10.2 for query totals; they are in §10.5.

## Why

The card's reason: the count is free for `list`, while an exact count for `grep` (b) would mean scanning every file. For the card
as a whole: each rule gives one meaning in every language and engine, which is what portable is for.

## Applied in

Draft v0.9 (#73, `75c8728`), §11.2 and §12.2; the filesystem backend followed in #75 (`1cb8f07`), with exact `list` counts.
