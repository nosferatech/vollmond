## Question

A root that is not an object, such as `[1, 1e400]` or a YAML list, is `root-not-object` either way. Are its contents still
checked? The JSON parser (#69) looked inside meanwhile, reporting `root-not-object` and `number-not-representable` at `/0` for
`[1e400]`; the suite had only `[1, 2]`.

## Options

- **(a) Also look inside and report its other issues**, as the JSON parser does. Chosen, as recommended. The YAML parser is
  changed to match.

Other options: Not recorded.

## Decision

(a), with card N's option (a), accepting every recommendation. A root that is not an object is `root-not-object`, and its contents
are still checked and reported (§4.1, §5.4, Appendix D), as [C2](C2.md) and [L3](L3.md) ask, with a case.

## Why

The card's reason: it follows C2 and L3, one run showing everything to fix. For the card as a whole: each rule follows YAML or C2
where they speak, and picks the safe reading where they don't.

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.

## Applied in

Draft v0.9 (#73, `75c8728`), §4.1, §5.4 and Appendix D; the YAML parser (#72, `24689e7`) was changed to match the JSON parser.
