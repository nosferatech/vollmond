## Question

The plan puts the strict read mode (§9.5, [G7](G7.md)) in task I3.4, with `query`. All its inputs exist in I2. When does it come?

## Options

- **(a) In I2.** Chosen, as recommended. Core's mode in task I2.3, and `--strict` for `get`, `outline`, `ls` and `refs` in I2.6.
  §12.1 gains `--strict`; I3.4 loses it.
- **(b) As planned, in I3.4.** As stated.

## Decision

(a).

## Why

The card's reason, which is also the case against (b): "An agent reading a store that doesn't validate can use it before
queries exist." The design adds that its inputs all exist in I2.

Draft v0.11 (#87, `169c597`): §12.1 (`--strict`), the plan (I2.3, I2.6, I3.4).
