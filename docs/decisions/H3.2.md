## Question

§5.3 removes trailing whitespace from a `$body`. Which characters does "trailing whitespace" cover? The Markdown fixtures (#54,
question 2) asked with a body ending in U+00A0 (no-break space), U+3000 or a form feed before the final line break.

## Options

- **(a) Spaces, tabs and line breaks only, the characters of a CommonMark blank line.** Chosen, as recommended. A trailing
  U+00A0 stays.
- **(b) Unicode White_Space,** which §6.3 step 4 uses for slugs.

## Decision

(a), as card H3's option (a), accepting every recommendation. Trailing whitespace is spaces, tabs and line breaks only, so a
trailing no-break space is content (§5.3, §5.8).

## Why

The PR's reason: a no-break space is content in Markdown, and (a) keeps `$body` independent of the Unicode version, which (b)
would not. The card's note for all sixteen items: each recommendation keeps vmd strict and its output readable back, and none
contradicts an earlier decision.
