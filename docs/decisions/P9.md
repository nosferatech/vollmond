## Question

§5.9's span table names `$tags` as a whole. What is the range of each `$tags` item taken from `<a class="…">`? The Markdown parser
(#79, its eighth open reading) gives each item its token's range in the `class` value.

## Options

- **(a) Its token in the attribute value**, as a row in §5.9's span table. Chosen, as recommended.

Other options: Not recorded.

## Decision

(a), as part of card P's option (a), accepting every recommendation: each `$tags` item from `<a class>` has as its range its token
in the attribute value, and §5.9's span table gets the row.

## Why

The card's reason for card P as a whole: it "picks the reading that loses no data silently". No reason specific to this item is
recorded.

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
