## Question

For `#same/$body`, where `same` names two sections, should `address-ambiguous` list the two sections, the step that matched
several, or the two `$body` nodes? The addresses (#83, its second open question) list the matches of the step that had several:
for `#same/same` that is Alpha and Beta, though as a selector only Beta remains.

## Options

- **(a) The matches of the step that matched several**, with a message naming that step. Chosen, as recommended.
- **(b) The nodes the whole address would select**, here the two `$body` nodes.

## Decision

(a), as part of card R's option (a), accepting every recommendation: `address-ambiguous` lists the matches of the step that matched
several, with a message naming that step.

## Why

The card's reason: evaluation stops at that step, and choosing one of those nodes is what the user must do. Card R as a whole
"matches what the code already does and keeps results the same across implementations".

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
