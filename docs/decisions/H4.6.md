## Question

What is `## Title fifteen <a id="a15"></a><a id="b15"></a>`, a heading with two anchor elements? §6.2 speaks of "the" element,
and `$anchor` holds one string. The derived anchor, `title-fifteen`, is clear either way. (#55, question 6.)

## Options

- **(a) A structural error (`anchor-element-invalid`),** one element per heading. Chosen, as recommended.

Other options: Not recorded.

## Decision

(a), as card H4's option (a), accepting every recommendation. Two anchor elements in one heading are a structural error,
`anchor-element-invalid` (§6.2).

## Why

The card's reason: `$anchor` holds one name. The card's note for the six items: each recommendation keeps vmd strict and its
output readable back, and none contradicts an earlier decision.

## Applied in

Draft v0.5 (#57, `d0d1424`), §6.2. I1.6 later made two `<a id>` at the start of one block the same error, and P4 an anchor
element nested inside emphasis or a link in a heading.
