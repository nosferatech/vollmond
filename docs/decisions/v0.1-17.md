## Question

The review asked: should vmd become Belfry's storage model, sit beside it, or should Belfry become a vmd backend?

## Options

- **(a) vmd becomes Belfry's storage model.**
- **(b) vmd sits beside Belfry.**
- **(c) Belfry becomes a vmd backend.**
- **(d) Belfry becomes a customer of vmd.** Chosen, from the owner's answer.

## Decision

(d). Belfry would become a customer of vmd. Perf results (today JSON plus a set of files) might be offered through a
vmd-compatible query or bundle interface, for uniformity.

Not recorded.
