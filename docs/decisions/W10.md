## Question

The card, "Details of links and the strict read mode that no card settled": "The last drafts (v0.17, v0.18) and pull requests
filled in these details of your AP to AU answers. Each is labelled "Agent's reading, for the owner to confirm". Confirming makes
them decided; any you reject becomes its own card. Ones a user would notice are marked (cost)." The card lists nine; the records
or documents that hold them are added in parentheses:

1. **A first step that names nothing makes the whole link `dangling`, whatever follows** (AP). In a repeating journal,
   `#tpyo/later` is `dangling`, like `#tpyo`. ([AP](AP.md))
2. **When only a field name in the record brings two unequal typed-reference declarations together** (a `$ref` object and its
   member, via two patterns), the object gets a schema error and neither declaration applies (AR). AR's card spoke only of
   refusing the schema. ([AR](AR.md))
3. **My reading of your AS note:** vmd does not refuse a record in strict mode for a typed link to a forbidden target, and the
   draft calls that the implementation's choice. `vmd check` reports it. ([AS](AS.md), the engineering manager's reading)
4. **`vmd check --fix` leaves a many-valued link into a moved record that found nothing as written** (AU), since it isn't
   `aliased`. ([AU](AU.md), its reading)
5. **After `vmd alias prune`, such a link goes straight from `ok` to `ref-dangling`, with no warning before** (cost; a consequence
   of AU, now stated in §8.5). (AU, the engineering manager's context)
6. **Why §9.3's example of `anchors: explicit` with many targets is a YAML record:** the card's Markdown example had a duplicate
   anchor (AM). ([AM](AM.md), reading 1)
7. **AO supersedes AD** in the decision records, since AD said the proof works from the schema alone. ([AO](AO.md), reading 3)
8. **`Store.binding` (library API) returns the same in both read modes** (from #146). (#146's reading 5, named in [W7](W7.md))
9. **The counts of links into each section** (outline's refs-in counts) count only `ok` and `aliased` links, each once per
   section; an ambiguous link's candidates don't count (R22, from #153). (the I2 design's reading R22)

## Options

- **(a) Confirm all nine.** Chosen, as recommended. The card: "The labels come off in the next draft. Nothing in the code
  changes."
- **(b) Confirm, except what the note says.** The card: "Each exception becomes its own card."
- **(c) Discuss first.** The card: "The readings stay labelled."

## Decision

(a): all nine readings are confirmed. The records that hold them, AP, AR, AS, AU, AM, AO and W7, say so, and so does the I2
design's readings table for R22.

## Why

The card's reason: "Each follows the safe side or what single links already do. Item 3 is my reading of your note on AS; item 5 is
the cost you accepted on AU, put in front of you once."

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
