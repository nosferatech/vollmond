## Question

An empty data block gives no fields ([H3.5](H3.5.md)). What does a `json data` block holding only white space give? The Markdown
parser (#79, its eleventh open reading) reads it as empty, as in YAML.

## Options

- **(a) No fields**, as an empty block gives. Chosen, as recommended.

Other options: Not recorded.

## Decision

(a), as part of card P's option (a), accepting every recommendation: a `json data` block of only white space gives no fields.

## Why

The card's reason: it is read "as an empty block does" (H3.5).

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
