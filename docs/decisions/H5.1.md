## Question

Step 1 of the slug rule (§6.3) did not say what a line break in a heading contributes: a soft break in a two-line setext heading,
or a hard break. The slug fixtures (#55, question 1) left out four captured headings over it. A line feed gives
`setext-five-line-one-setext-five-line-two`; nothing gives `setext-five-line-onesetext-five-line-two`, GitHub's result.

## Options

- **(a) A line break counts as white space, so a hyphen:** `setext-line-one-setext-line-two`. Chosen, as recommended. `<br>`
  gives nothing, as §5.3 already says for removed tags.
- **(b) A line break contributes nothing,** as GitHub gives: `…line-onesetext…`.

## Decision

(a), as card H5's option (a), accepting every recommendation. A soft or hard line break counts as white space, so it becomes a
hyphen. `<br>` gives nothing, as §5.3 already said (§6.3 step 1).

## Why

The PR's reason: nothing glues two words into one that nobody wrote, which is what (b) does. The card's note: each recommendation
keeps vmd strict and its output readable back, and none contradicts an earlier decision.

## Applied in

Draft v0.5 (#57, `d0d1424`), §6.3 step 1; the review of #57 corrected that a line break becomes a hyphen through steps 5 and 6,
not step 5 alone. Q1 later reads a JSON or YAML title's line breaks as soft breaks, as here.
