## Question

Question 9 of the phase I1 design (#59), item 9 of card K. Since `@vollmond/core` may use only the APIs every target runtime
provides, hashing (§11.3) is either asynchronous or a small implementation of its own. Which?

## Options

- **(a) `crypto.subtle`**, which is asynchronous and, in browsers, limited to secure contexts (MDN, `SubtleCrypto`). Chosen, as
  recommended.
- **(b) A small synchronous implementation.**

## Decision

(a), through card K's option (b), which accepted every recommendation but question 3's: hashing with `crypto.subtle`, so
`nodeVersion` and `gitBlobId` are asynchronous. It is an implementation choice that the design records, not a change to the
specification.

## Why

The design's reason: every read is asynchronous anyway. The card adds that in browsers it is only available on HTTPS pages.

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
