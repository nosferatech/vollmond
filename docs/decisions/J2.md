## Question

Item 2 of card J, from the independent review of #58, and question 14 of the phase I1 design (#59), which card K left to card J.
[I1.7](I1.7.md) puts block anchors on "any list item at any depth", which literally includes a list item inside a block quote,
while Draft v0.5 excluded every container. Can a list item inside a block quote carry a block anchor?

## Options

- **(a) Yes, by the words "any depth".** The design's option (a).
- **(b) No: block quotes stay prose, as for headings inside them ([H3.11](H3.11.md)), and "at any depth" means list nesting.**
  Chosen, as recommended by the card and the design.

## Decision

(b), through card J's option (a), accepting every recommendation: a list item inside a block quote carries no block anchor. Block
quotes stay prose, and "at any depth" in I1.7 means list nesting. This closes the open I1.7 reading (§6.2).

## Why

The card's reason: block quotes stay prose, as for headings inside them (H3.11). The design's reason: "so that a block quote
means the same for headings, paragraphs and list items", since everything inside a block quote is prose, as a paragraph there
already is.

## Applied in

Draft v0.7 (#61, `c3032a6`), §6.2; the phase I1 design records it as the answer to its question 14 (#62).
