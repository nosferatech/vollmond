## Question

`get --meta all` silently drops `@refs` and `@collection` under `--json`, so a field that is absent and one that is not computed
yet look the same. In text they are marked "(not computed yet)", and the JSON leaves them out without an issue, since no code in
Appendix D fits (#84, its eighth open question). What does a read give for them?

## Options

- **(a) Until I2 computes them, list them with `null` and a warning issue naming them.** Chosen, as recommended.
- **(b) Keep #84's interim reading.** They are left out of the JSON without an issue, and look absent.

## Decision

(a), as part of card S's option (a), accepting every recommendation. The card names no issue code for the warning.

**Agent's reading, confirmed by the owner on [W3](W3.md) (item 3).** A computed field that does not apply is absent, not null, as
`@key` is for the root: `@collection` is absent for a record in no collection, and for one in a `collection-conflict`
([U5](U5.md)), which is read unschematized. Draft v0.12 states it in §5.10, as #91 implements it.

## Why

The card's reason: under (b), absent and not computed look the same. Card S as a whole keeps the output "exact for programs".
