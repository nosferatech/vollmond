## Question

§5.10 defines only `@range` for a block anchor. A block anchor is not a section, so it has no `@key` or `@depth`, and its `@source`
is its block, by §5.9. What are its `@version` and `@issues`? The addresses (#83, its fourth open question) give `@version` as the
node version of the block's text as a string, and `@issues` as the `$body`'s issues at offsets inside the block.

## Options

- **(a) `@version` hashes the block's text; `@issues` are the `$body`'s issues within the block's range**, the readings of #83.
  Chosen, as recommended.

Other options: Not recorded.

## Decision

(a), as part of card R's option (a), accepting every recommendation: a block anchor has no `@key` or `@depth`, its `@source` is its
block, its `@version` hashes the block's text, and its `@issues` are the `$body`'s issues within the block's range.

## Why

The card's reason: both are the code's readings, and they matter for edits in I4. Card R as a whole "matches what the code already
does and keeps results the same across implementations".

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
