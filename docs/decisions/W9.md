## Question

The card: "Typed references (PR #149, merged) filled in these details of your AL, AM and AN answers. They are readings R14 to R21
in the I2 design, labelled for you to confirm. Confirming makes them decided; any you reject becomes its own card. Ones a user
would notice are marked (cost)." [AL](AL.md), [AM](AM.md) and [AN](AN.md) are those answers. The card lists eight:

1. **Allowed targets are checked whenever the link reaches something** (R14). The card: "`ok`, `aliased`, `ambiguous`,
   `unreadable`, and an empty many-valued link; not for a `dangling` one. The record checked is the one after any rename of the
   record."
2. **A record two collections claim matches neither** (R15). The card: "in a `targets` list, as it is read as belonging to none."
3. **`targets: []` allows nothing** (R16). The card: "leaving `targets` out allows anything."
4. **Under `anchors: explicit`, a link through an old anchor name is checked after the rename** (R17). The card: "if the alias
   leads to a title-derived name, it's refused."
5. **Under `anchors: explicit`, a step through a title raises both** (R18). The card: "the refusal (`ref-target-not-allowed`) and
   the warning (`ref-derived-anchor`)."
6. **A many-valued link through an old name that then finds nothing is `ok` with no targets** (R19). The card: "`#setup/nothing`,
   with `setup` renamed to `install` and nothing called `nothing` below it: a single link there stays `dangling`, so the
   many-valued one is `ok` and empty. The other reading is `aliased` with no targets. Draft v0.16's §8.5 reads the other way and
   is aligned in the next draft."
7. **Links outside the store aren't checked against `targets` (cost)** (R20). The card: "vmd tracks nothing outside the store, so
   a field limited to `targets: [tickets]` accepts `https://…` and `../../elsewhere.md` above the store's root."
8. **Until you answer AR, a `$ref` object's and its member's declarations are compared exactly as written** (R21). The card:
   "`{}` and `{cardinality: one}` count as different and are refused, as they are when both sit at one place."

## Options

- **(a) Confirm all eight.** Chosen, as recommended. The card: "The labels come off in the next draft; item 6 changes §8.5's
  wording. Nothing in the code changes."
- **(b) Confirm, except what the note says.** The card: "Each exception becomes its own card."
- **(c) Discuss first.** The card: "The readings stay labelled."

## Decision

(a): readings R14 to R21 of the I2 design are confirmed. The design's readings table says so for each, and Draft v0.17 states
them in §8.5 (item 6) and §9.3 (the others). [AR](AR.md), answered the same day, refuses unequal declarations on a `$ref` object
and its member, compared as item 8 says.

**Agent's reading, for the owner to confirm.** R19 in the design also says that "a record alias makes a `many` reference
`aliased`, with no targets where its fragment matches nothing in the record it leads to"; the card's item 6 summarises only the
anchor alias. Draft v0.17 states both halves in §8.5, reading the card's "They are readings R14 to R21" as confirming each
reading whole.

## Why

The card's reason: "Each follows the safe side or mirrors what a single link does. Item 7 is the one to look at twice: refusing
links that leave the store would need a rule for what a URL may be, which no decision has; today a schema author who wants that
can add a `pattern` beside the declaration."

Why (b) and (c) lost: Not recorded.

## Owner's note

The project owner chose (a) on the review desk on 2026-10-20, with no note.
