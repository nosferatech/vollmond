## Question

Item 2 of card Y, from the pull request that implements cards P, Q, R and T (#94) and its independent review, which found
nothing blocking. [P1](P1.md) limits a paragraph or table cell to 4,096 runs, so headings and data `$title`s are exempt from the
run limit. Their emphasis costs the same: a `$title` of 20,000 `*a*` takes 0.82 s and is never limited. Do their runs count?

## Options

- **(a) Count runs in headings and in data `$title`s too.** Chosen, as recommended.
- **(b) Accept, except what the note says**, card Y's option (b): "The exceptions keep today's behaviour or get their own card."
  Today, headings and data `$title`s are exempt from the run limit.

## Decision

(a), as part of card Y's option (a), accepting all three: the runs of a heading and of a data `$title` count toward the limit of
4,096 runs.

**Agent's reading, confirmed by the owner on [W3](W3.md) (items 6 and 8).** Each heading and each data `$title` is a unit of its
own, with the same bound of 4,096 as a paragraph or a table cell, rather than counting with a neighbouring paragraph; and passing
it is a `syntax-error` at the record's root, as P1's other limits are in #94. That this amends P1, and so supersedes it, is also
this record's reading.

## Why

The card's reason for the item: their emphasis costs the same, and a `$title` of 20,000 `*a*` takes 0.82 s and is never limited.
For the card as a whole: it "closes two cheap gaps".

Why (b) lost: Not recorded.
