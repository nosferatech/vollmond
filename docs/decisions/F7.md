## Question

On [C20](C20.md) the owner expected selectors to need and, or and not, and a distinction between filtering, projection with
descent, and a final projection, and that this is a long discussion. The session agreed. Nothing before I2.4 (references) needs
a reference that selects by tag, and VQL already filters by `@tags`. Defer tag selection to a selector-language design?

## Options

- **(a) Defer, and open a selector-language design issue.** Chosen, as recommended. Until that design lands, addresses do not
  select by tag, VQL's `@tags` does, and a many-reference lists its targets. The issue starts from prior art (CSS selectors,
  JSONPath RFC 9535, XPath, jq) and runs before I4.
- **(b) Adopt `.tag` now as an interim form.** References can select by tag from I2.4 on, at the risk that the later design
  replaces the form.

## Decision

(a). Tag selection is deferred to a selector-language design issue (#47), to run before I4. It covers and, or and not, filtering
versus projection with descent, and a final projection, and starts from CSS selectors, JSONPath (RFC 9535), XPath and jq. Until
then addresses do not select by tag, VQL's `@tags` does, and a many-reference lists its targets.

## Why

The card's reason: it avoids committing to syntax the selector design may replace, which is the risk of (b).
