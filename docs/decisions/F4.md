## Question

The owner's note on [C7](C7.md) raised something the spec barely covered. Stores are configured at different times against
different versions of the format; data written under one version stays; clients built against version X meet stores at version
Y. The owner suggested that a client declares its version (in the repository's settings, or in a connection string), its payload
is validated against X and transcoded to Y, and reads are transcoded back. The spec had only `vmd: 1` in `.vmd/config.yaml` and a
spec version that rises with each decision round ([C27](C27.md)); transcoding needs version-to-version mappings that do not exist
until a second format version does.

## Options

- **(a) Design issue now, with a small interim rule.** Chosen, as recommended. A design issue is opened and discussed before I4
  (writes). Until then, a store declares its format version (`vmd: 1`), a client refuses to write to a store whose version it does
  not support and warns when it reads one, and the spec reserves room for client-declared versions.
- **(b) Design versioning and transcoding now.** Settled before the fixtures, but designed without a second version to test
  against, and it delays I0.8.

## Decision

(a). A design issue (#46), discussed before I4. The interim rule is that a store declares its format version (`vmd: 1`), that a
client refuses to write to a store whose version it does not support and warns when it reads one, and that the spec reserves
room for client-declared versions, in the repository's settings or in a connection string. The owner's model for the design is
that a client declares its version, its payload is validated against that version and transcoded to the store's, and reads are
transcoded back.

## Why

The card's reason: the interim rule makes mismatches visible and safe, and the real design can be tested once a second version
exists. (b) designs without a second version to test against and delays I0.8.

## Applied in

Draft v0.4 (#50, `1043a6c`), §9.1, and issue #46. [G3](G3.md), from the review of #50, replaced the interim warning on reading a
store whose version the client does not support: a newer store is refused.
