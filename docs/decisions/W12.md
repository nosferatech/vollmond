## Question

The card, "Four older details from earlier rounds that were never confirmed": "Collecting the open readings turned up four in
older decision records, from rounds H, J and O, that say "the agent's reading" and were never put to you. Confirming makes them
decided; any you reject becomes its own card." The card lists four; the records or documents that hold them are added in
parentheses:

1. **The warning code `unicode-runtime-older` and its name** (O4). Your card asked for a warning when Node's Unicode is older than
   vmd's, as an issue, but named no code; the draft added this one. ([O4](O4.md))
2. **How a decimal written loosely is normalized** (H2.7a). vmd accepts XML Schema's decimal forms (`.5`, `+1`, `1.`, `01`),
   applies no whitespace collapsing first, and writes them back as `0.5`, `1`, `1`, `1`, keeping trailing zeros (`1.50`) and
   writing `-0` as `0`. ([H2.7a](H2.7a.md))
3. **`Null` and `NULL` are quoted in YAML as null look-alikes** (H2.8), since YAML 1.1 and 1.2 both read them as null; Draft
   v0.4's list had missed them. ([H2.8](H2.8.md))
4. **A date-time without an offset is taken as UTC for the leap-second check** (J1), as the main JavaScript validator does.
   ([J1](J1.md))

## Options

- **(a) Confirm all four.** The card: "The labels come off in the next draft. Nothing in the code changes."
- **(b) Confirm, except what the note says.** Chosen. The card: "Each exception becomes its own card."
- **(c) Discuss first.** The card: "The readings stay labelled."

## Decision

(b): items 1 to 3 are confirmed, and the records that hold them, O4, H2.7a and H2.8, say so. Item 4, J1's reading that a local
date-time is taken as UTC for the leap-second check, became card [AV](AV.md), which the owner answered with its option (c): a
local date-time accepts `:60` in any minute.

## Why

The card's reason: "Each has been in the code and the draft for several rounds without trouble, and each follows the standard it
cites."

Why (a) lost: for item 4, the owner's note below. Why (c) lost: Not recorded.

## Owner's note

The project owner chose (b) on the review desk on 2026-10-20, with the note:

> Not sure about the leap second parting as UTC. Can we tell how the time will be interpreted by the actual parser? Like is it possible that the time after parse may get reinterpreted as different time zone? Ideally we shouldn’t be involved as little as possible in time and date parsing and just use standard library. Given cross platform the standard library may not be well defined. So is the case here that we need to declare the specific parsing rules for the format?

The engineering manager answered the questions in the conversation and on card AV, whose background the AV record quotes.

## Applied in

Draft v0.19: the records of O4, H2.7a and H2.8, whose readings now say that they are confirmed; [AV](AV.md)'s record and §4.3
for item 4. The draft states items 1 to 3 as rules already, so its text keeps them.
