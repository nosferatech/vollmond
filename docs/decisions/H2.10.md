## Question

Item 10 of card H2, from #52 Q9. §4.3 did not state int64's range; #52's cases assumed [−2^63, 2^63−1], from the name and from
"bigint: as int64, unbounded". What is int64's range?

## Options

- **(a) The signed 64-bit range, as the OpenAPI format registry defines it; §4.3 says so.** Chosen, as recommended.

Other options: Not recorded.

## Decision

(a), with card H2's option (a), accepting every recommendation. int64 is the signed 64-bit range, [−2^63, 2^63−1], per the
OpenAPI format registry (§4.3).

## Why

The reasons in #52 Q9: the name, and §4.3's "bigint: as int64, unbounded"; the card cites the OpenAPI format registry's
definition. Card H2's reason for accepting every recommendation: each keeps vmd strict and its output readable back, and none
contradicts an earlier decision.
