## Question

`$id` is valid JSON Schema 2020-12, and §9.2 says schemas are 2020-12 documents. But a file's own base URI changes what its
relative `$ref`s mean, and no schema in the suite or in Appendix A needs it. Is `$id` allowed in a schema file?

## Options

- **(a) Refused in I2 (`schema-invalid`).** Chosen, as recommended. §9.2 says that vmd's schemas are 2020-12 without `$id`.
  Allowing it later is an addition.
- **(b) Allowed.** Every file stays reachable by its name as well as by its `$id`.

## Decision

(a), for now: §9.2 says that vmd's schemas are 2020-12 without `$id`, and one is `schema-invalid`.

## Why

The card's reason: "Simpler resolution now, and reversible by a minor version." The design's case against (b): a base URI of
its own changes what a relative `$ref` in that file means, and no schema of the suite or of Appendix A needs one; allowing it
later is an addition under [U21](U21.md).
