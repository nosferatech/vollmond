## Question

What does `--quiet` remove? The CLI foundation (#74) dropped warnings under `--quiet` meanwhile.

## Options

- **(a) Warnings only, including the Unicode runtime warning.** Chosen, as recommended. Errors and the result are always printed.

Other options: Not recorded.

## Decision

(a), with card O's option (a), accepting every recommendation. `--quiet` leaves out warnings only, the warning about the runtime's
Unicode data included, on stderr and from the JSON object alike, except for `check`, whose warnings are its result; errors and
the result are always printed (§12.1).

## Why

The card's reason for the card as a whole: each rule makes the CLI predictable for agents and keeps anchors stable across Node
versions.

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.

Draft v0.10 (#78, `d1e0a60`), §12.1; the CLI follows it in #81 (`df83220`).
