## Question

§11.2's table of operations lacked the `cursor` that §12.2's paging needs. The storage contract and filesystem backend (#68)
documented both cursors as opaque and checked them, a malformed one being `query-invalid`.

## Options

- **(a) Add `cursor` to `list` and `grep`, and say cursors are opaque**, as the draft already said for queries. Chosen, as
  recommended.

Other options: Not recorded.

## Decision

(a), with card M's option (a), accepting every recommendation. §11.2's table gives `grep` a `cursor`, as `list` already had, and
cursors are opaque.

## Why

The card's reason for the card as a whole: each rule gives one meaning in every language and engine, which is what portable is
for.

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
