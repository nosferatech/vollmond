## Question

Item 5 of card H2, from #52 Q7. An alias to an undefined anchor, `a: *nope`, is an error under YAML 1.2.2, section 7.1, and the
`yaml` package throws `ReferenceError` from `toJS`. Is it `syntax-error` or `yaml-alias`?

## Options

- **(a) `yaml-alias` at `/a`.** Chosen, as recommended.
- **(b) `syntax-error`**, the other reading #52 Q7 names.

## Decision

(a), with card H2's option (a), accepting every recommendation. An alias to an undefined anchor is `yaml-alias` at the alias's
location (Appendix D).

## Why

The reason in #52 Q7: vmd rejects every alias anyway, and that code also gives the location. Card H2's reason for accepting every
recommendation: each keeps vmd strict and its output readable back, and none contradicts an earlier decision.
