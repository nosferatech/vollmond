## Question

§9.3 gives `x-vmd-summary` no value type.

## Options

- **(a) A boolean; anything else `schema-invalid`.** Chosen, as recommended. `x-vmd-summary: false` means what it says.
- **(b) Any value counts as true.** As stated.

## Decision

(a). §9.3's table gives the value.

## Why

The card gives no reason of its own; its option (a) says "`x-vmd-summary: false` means what it says", and the design recommends
(a) "so that `x-vmd-summary: false` means what it says".

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
