## Question

`vmd get` prints a node's Markdown as is, so a record holding terminal escape sequences or bidi controls can retitle the terminal
or reorder what is shown. It matters more once stores can be remote. The point came from the independent review of the read
commands (#84), as a safety point. What does `get` print to a terminal?

## Options

- **(a) Escape in text output to a terminal.** Chosen, as recommended. `get` escapes control and bidi characters except line feed
  and tab; piped output and `--json` are unchanged, and `cat` always prints raw bytes. That is what #84 now does as an interim.
- **(b) Keep the draft as it is.** `get` prints the node's Markdown as is, and a record can retitle the terminal or reorder what is
  shown.

## Decision

(a), as part of card S's option (a), accepting every recommendation.

## Why

The card's reason: card S keeps the output "safe for a terminal, cheap in tokens, and exact for programs". The card's statement
of the problem, against (b): a record can retitle the terminal or reorder what is shown, and "It matters more once stores can be
remote."
