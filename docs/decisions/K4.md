## Question

Question 4 of the phase I1 design (#59), item 4 of card K. §7.5's form 3 is reached "when the path of form 2 is not singular
(§7.4: a level that allows repeats by `multimap` or `lenient`, …)". Does lenient mode alone make form 2 non-singular?

## Options

- **(a) Lenient mode makes form 2 non-singular only where evaluation meets a duplicate, as §7.4 lists lenient mode among the
  cases that fail at evaluation.** Chosen, as recommended, with a wording fix to §7.5.
- **(b) Lenient mode alone makes form 2 non-singular at every level no schema declares**, so a lenient store's canonical
  addresses fall to derived anchors.

## Decision

(a), through card K's option (b), which accepted every recommendation but question 3's. Lenient mode makes form 2 non-singular
only where evaluation meets a duplicate, as §7.4 lists it among the cases that fail at evaluation; §7.5's wording is fixed.

## Why

The design's reason: (a) agrees with §7.4 and with the suite's strict and lenient twin cases. The design states (b)'s consequence,
that a lenient store's canonical addresses would fall to derived anchors.

## Applied in

Draft v0.7 (#61, `c3032a6`), §7.5.
