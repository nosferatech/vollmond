## Question

§8.1 makes a typed reference a string whose schema has both `format: uri-reference` and `x-vmd-ref`, and does not say what
`x-vmd-ref` alone means.

## Options

- **(a) `schema-invalid`.** Chosen, as recommended. A declaration that does nothing is reported.
- **(b) No reference, silently.** As stated.

## Decision

(a).

## Why

The card's reason, which is also the case against (b): "A declaration that does nothing is a mistake."

Draft v0.11 (#87, `169c597`): §9.3, Appendix D `schema-invalid`.
