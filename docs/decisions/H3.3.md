## Question

CommonMark (section 4.3) makes `First line`, `  second line` and a `----` underline one level-2 heading. The same happens when an
author forgets a blank line: in `Root text.`, `Part`, `---`, the text "Root text." becomes part of the heading. What is the
`$title` of such a heading? (#54, question 3.)

## Options

- **(a) The source lines,** `"First line\n  second line"`.
- **(b) The lines without their indentation, joined by `\n`,** `"First line\nsecond line"`, which is the heading's content as
  CommonMark (section 4.8) defines a paragraph's. Chosen, as recommended. `check` warns that the title cannot be written back as
  one line (`not-representable`).
- **(c) The lines joined by a space.**
- **(d) A structural error.**

## Decision

(b), as card H3's option (a), accepting every recommendation. A multi-line setext heading has as `$title` its lines without their
indentation, joined by `\n`, and `vmd check` warns `not-representable`. For the warning to hold at the root too, §5.8's
single-line rule now covers every `$title`, the root's included, where it named only the items of `$sections`.

## Why

The PR's reason: §5.8 already refuses a multi-line `$title`, so `check` reports `not-representable`, and a warning for a setext
heading of several lines catches the forgotten blank line. Why the other options lost: Not recorded. The card's note for all
sixteen items: each recommendation keeps vmd strict and its output readable back, and none contradicts an earlier decision.
