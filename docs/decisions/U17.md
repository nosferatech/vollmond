## Question

§9.3 allows `@key` only on `$sections`, but does not forbid other keys there.

## Options

- **(a) `$sections` is keyed by `["@key"]` only.** Chosen, as recommended. Sections are keyed by their section key by definition
  (§5.6).
- **(b) Other keys allowed.** Sections could be keyed by a field.

## Decision

(a): a `$sections` list is keyed by `["@key"]` only; §9.3 says so.

## Why

The card's reason: "Matches §5.6's definition."

Why the other options lost: Not recorded.
