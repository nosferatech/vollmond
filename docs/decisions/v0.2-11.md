## Question

`$anchor` stays unique per record. The session proposed a separate form for labels that may repeat, with HTML as the precedent
(`id` is unique and `#id` selects one element; `class` is not, and `.class` selects a set), and asked whether `$class` is the
right name.

## Options

- **(a) `$class`**, the session's first name for it.
- **(b) `$tags`.** Chosen, by the owner.

In Markdown, the session wrote tags as the HTML `class` attribute in the heading's anchor element, and offered `data-tags="…"` as
the other HTML option if `class` was to be avoided there too.

## Decision

(b). `$anchor` is unique per record. Non-unique labels are `$tags` (not `$class` or `$type`, which serialization frameworks
commonly use). Tags select sets of nodes, through VQL (`@tags:example`) or references declared `cardinality: many`. In Markdown
they are written with the HTML idiom, `<a class="example"></a>` in a heading, and map to `$tags`.

## Why

The owner's reason against `$class` (and `$type`): serialization frameworks might easily use them. Why `data-tags` lost to
`class` in Markdown: Not recorded.

## Owner's note

> 2. I'd use $tags. $class or $type might easily be used by serialization frameworks.
